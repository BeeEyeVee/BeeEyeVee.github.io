Frequently asked

How do I download my language?

The spoken meditations and stories for your language download from Apple the first time you need them. On the Today screen, tap Download in the banner at the top. Slumia asks first if you are on mobile data, because a language is about 100 to 130 MB.

How do I switch language, and how do I remove one?

Open Settings (the gear on the Today screen) and choose App language. Under Languages on this device you can see each downloaded language and remove it to free space. A language that is playing right now cannot be removed until you pause or stop it.

Does Slumia work without an internet connection?

Yes, once your language has downloaded. The nature soundscapes are inside the app and always work offline.

A download will not finish. What should I do?

Check that you have a connection and enough free space, then tap Try again in the banner. Downloads continue in the background, so you can leave the app. If it still fails, email me with your iOS version.

How do I set a bedtime reminder?

Settings, then Daily reminder. Choose a time and the days to repeat on. If iOS notifications are turned off for Slumia, turn them on in the iOS Settings app.

How do I add the widget?

Press and hold the Home Screen, tap +, and search for Slumia. The widget and the Control Centre control start a nature soundscape without opening the app.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone only. Slumia collects nothing and has no account. See the Privacy Policy.
