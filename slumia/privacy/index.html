Slumia

Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026

Slumia is a sleep app. It does not collect your data, has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and does not track you. What you do in the app stays on your iPhone.

What Slumia collects

Nothing. The developer cannot see which tracks you play, when you use the app, your reminder times, or anything else about you. There is no Slumia server, no account and no sign-in, and the app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising code.

What is stored on your device

Slumia keeps the following on your iPhone only, and it never leaves it:

  • your language choice and your daily reminder settings;
  • your favourites and where you stopped in a track;
  • the daily affirmation chosen for you, and a small snapshot the Slumia widget uses to show its title.

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Language downloads

The spoken meditations and stories for each language are not inside the app; they are downloaded when you choose a language. Apple hosts these files and Apple's own download service fetches them when Slumia asks. Slumia does not run the download, does not operate a server for it, and does not receive any information about it. Apple's handling of the download is described in Apple's privacy policy. The nature soundscapes are inside the app and need no download. Once a language is on your device, it works without an internet connection.

Notifications

If you turn on the bedtime reminder, Slumia asks iOS for permission to send notifications. The reminder is scheduled on your iPhone by iOS. It does not use push notifications and nothing about it is sent to the developer.

Tracking and third parties

Slumia does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, does not share data with anyone, and does not include third-party software.

Children

Slumia collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date, before an app update that changes what Slumia does with data.
