Common questions

How do I save something into Snip?

Use the Share button in Safari, Photos, Files or any other app and choose Snip. Pick a snip, add a note or tags, and tap Save.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Saving never waits for the network, and saved pages are archived on your device.

How do I lock a snip?

Open the snip's menu, choose Edit and turn on the lock. Locked snips need Face ID or your passcode and never appear in search or the share sheet.

My snips are not on my other device.

Check that iCloud is on for Snip in Settings → your name → iCloud, and look at the sync card in Snip's Settings. Very large files stay on the device that saved them.

How do I move a snip to another device?

Open the snip's menu and choose Share Snip. Send it with AirDrop or Messages and open it in Snip on the other device.
